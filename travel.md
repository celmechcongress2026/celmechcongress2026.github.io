Participants traveling from Mexico City can reach Oaxaca via flights to the Aeropuerto Internacional de Oaxaca (OAX / Xoxocotlán). Departures from Mexico City are exclusively from the Aeropuerto Internacional Benito Juárez (MEX).  

---

## ✈️ Flights from Mexico City

Several airlines operate this route:  
- AeroMéxico (Terminal 2)  
- Volaris (Terminal 1)  
- VivaAerobus (Terminal 1)  

Flight duration is approximately 1 hour and 15–30 minutes, covering around 370 km (229 mi).

---

## 🚖 From Oaxaca Airport to the City

The Oaxaca airport is located about 15 minutes from the city center. Options to reach your accommodation include:  
- Taxi: Available outside the arrivals area  
- Rideshare apps: Uber, DiDi, etc.  
- Shuttle / Private transfer: Can be arranged in advance by hotels or the conference organizers  

> Tip: Confirm your terminal and check your airline for the latest schedule before travel.
